Which one is Mars?

Ten ways to draw Mars for the desktop app, all based on the new photos. Each card shows the drawing large and at the real size it will have on your screen.

  • Very dark chocolate coat
  • Shaggy, wavy fur
  • Long beard with golden-brown tips
  • Fringe over light amber eyes
  • Big dark nose
  • Long, low ears
  • Lean and long-legged
  • Pink-red harness with a MARS tag

How to choose: tell Claude the number you like best. You can also mix, for example "the face of 2 with the coat of 3", or ask for changes such as lighter beard tips or a different pose. The chosen style then replaces the current dog in the app, with all moods and activities.
